Controls:
Take order - touch customer on left
Give order - drag finished dish to customer on right
Add ingredients to pot - drag ingredients into the pot
Make meal - press the make meal button and head to the counter to view
Transition - press button in bottom left corner
View orders - press button in top right corner

Features Present:
Take a customers order
Make a meal:
	- making recipe wrong = bad meal
	- making recipe right = good meal

Give customer meal:
	-Checks if meal is correct based on order
